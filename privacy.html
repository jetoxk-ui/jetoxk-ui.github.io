GI-World

Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026.

Who uses GI-World

GI-World is a private tool run by one owner on their own computer. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

What it accesses

Only the owner's own accounts, and only after the owner signs in and allows it on each platform's own consent screen:

  • YouTube (Google): upload videos to the owner's channels, read those channels' names and their videos' views, and read YouTube Analytics for those channels.
  • TikTok: post videos to the owner's account and read its basic profile and video statistics.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): publish videos to the owner's Instagram professional account and Facebook Page, and read their basic insights.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens, videos and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. GI-World runs no server that collects data, and it does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone. It uses no data for advertising.

Google user data

GI-World's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and in TikTok's and Facebook's settings under connected or business apps. Deleting GI-World's folder on the owner's computer deletes everything it stored.
